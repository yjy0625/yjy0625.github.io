Main Post

How can robots learn to carry out various periodic behaviors in our everyday life, like wiping tables, stirring food, and winding ropes? Check out our new approach ViPTL, which can learn to execute a periodic task from a single human demonstration!

Arxiv: arxiv.org/abs/2109.14078
Website: yjy0625.github.io/projects/viptl

No. 1

To represent and execute periodic manipulation tasks, we use rhythmic Dynamic Movement Primitives (rDMPs), which model cyclic motion, with shift parameters that account for motion that translates over multiple periods.

1/5

No. 2

Our approach uses periodicity estimation for segmenting the demo into repeating segments, visual representation learning for third-person imitation, and Bayesian optimization for efficiently searching for the rDMP that represents the right task motion.

2/5

No. 3

Within 50 trials, our approach can successfully learn periodic manipulation tasks involving deformable and granular objects, such as table wiping, rope winding, and food stirring.

3/5

No. 4

Our learned manipulation skills can be flexibly executed with different repetition count, speed, and amplitude, thanks to our rDMP representation of periodic manipulation policies.
